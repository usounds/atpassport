## 1. Information We Collect
This Service collects and stores the following information:

- atproto handles registered by users
- DID (Decentralized Identifier) associated with each handle
- PDS (Personal Data Server) URL information
- Session identifiers (stored as cookies)

## 2. How We Use Information
The collected information is used for:

- Providing identity resolution and authentication services
- Managing user-registered handles
- Facilitating authentication with third-party applications

## 3. Information Sharing
We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except when necessary to provide the Service (e.g., resolving DID information via the AT Protocol network).

## 4. Data Storage
Your data is stored securely using AWS DynamoDB. Session information is managed via signed JWT tokens.

## 5. Cookies
This Service uses session cookies to maintain your authenticated state. These cookies are essential for the functioning of this Service.

## 6. Data Deletion
You may delete your registered handles at any time through the Service interface. If you wish to have all your data completely removed, please contact us.

## 7. Contact
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact usounds.work.
